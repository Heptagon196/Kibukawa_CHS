Kibu2 Dialogue Pixel 16 - a subset/format conversion of GNU Unifont 16.0.04
Font homepage: https://unifoundry.com/unifont/
Original glyph designs: GNU Unifont contributors, including Roman Czyborra,
Paul Hardy, Qianqian Fang/Wen Quan Yi and other contributors credited
in Unifont-README.txt and the upstream source distribution.
The font and this derived atlas are distributed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1. See Unifont-OFL-1.1.txt and Unifont-COPYING.txt.
Only a subset selection and PNG/KBF2 conversion was performed; each
source bitmap pixel is preserved without resampling or antialiasing.
The unchanged original .hex.gz is included in fonts/.
